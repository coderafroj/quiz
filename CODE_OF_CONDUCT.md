# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be welcoming to everyone, especially students and first-time contributors.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient. Many contributors are learning.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent and ask questions before judging.
- Accept that maintainers may decline contributions that do not fit the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks
- Spam, self-promotion unrelated to the project, or trolling
- Publishing other people's private information
- Submitting copied or plagiarized content as your own

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests, and may block people who break these rules. Report problems by contacting the maintainer at the email listed on [github.com/coderafroj](https://github.com/coderafroj) or through a private message on GitHub.

This code is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
